Nothing you typed was read, stored, or transmitted anywhere. Only the fact that the demo form was submitted, with a timestamp, is logged for the course dashboard.

This was a phishing simulation

The page you just used was a test run by your IT & Security team, not a real corp.local login.

What gave it away, if you're looking back:
  • The email created urgency — "expires today" — to rush you into acting.
  • It asked you to log in through a link in an email, instead of going to the portal directly.
  • The page address didn't match where you'd normally sign in.

If this had been a real attack, whatever you typed would have gone straight to someone outside the company. Next time, type the portal address in yourself, or open it from a bookmark, rather than clicking a link in an email that's asking you to sign in urgently.
